# The 12-Movement Design Canon

Translate historical art, architectural, and design movements into exact digital styling tokens and rules.

---

## 1. Renaissance Classicism (Leonardo da Vinci, Leon Battista Alberti)
- **Philosophy:** Harmony, proportion, optics, Chiaroscuro light staging, Sfumato edges.
- **Palette:** Dark umber, warm limestone, gold leaf accent, deep atmospheric carbon.
- **Typography:** High-contrast classical serif (Cormorant Garamond, EB Garamond) paired with refined humanist sans.
- **Layout:** Root-2 and Golden Ratio proportions, pyramidal composition, contrapposto balance.

## 2. Bauhaus Modernism (Walter Gropius, Wassily Kandinsky, Johannes Itten)
- **Philosophy:** Form follows function, primary geometric elements (circle, square, triangle), truth to materials.
- **Palette:** Pure primary triad (cadmium red, cobalt blue, yellow) against stark white and architectural gray.
- **Typography:** Geometric sans-serif (Futura, Syne) with bold asymmetric hierarchy.
- **Layout:** Asymmetrical modular grid, heavy diagonal structural bars, unadorned planar surfaces.

## 3. Swiss International Typographic Style (Josef Müller-Brockmann, Massimo Vignelli)
- **Philosophy:** Objective clarity, mathematical grid discipline, zero decorative ornament.
- **Palette:** Strict monochrome (pure black, cool white, neutral gray) with a single vermilion red accent.
- **Typography:** Grotesk sans-serif (Helvetica Now, Neue Haas Grotesk, Akzidenz-Grotesk) set with mathematical tracking.
- **Layout:** Rigid 8-column or 12-column grid, ragged-right text, generous white margins.

## 4. De Stijl & Neoplasticism (Piet Mondrian, Gerrit Rietveld)
- **Philosophy:** Reduction to absolute essentials: vertical and horizontal lines, primary colors, non-colors.
- **Palette:** Pure red (`#e63946`), yellow (`#ffb703`), blue (`#1d3557`), black (`#111111`), white (`#ffffff`).
- **Typography:** Stark monospaced or rigid geometric sans.
- **Layout:** Thick black grid lines dividing asymmetric rectangular color planes.

## 5. Japanese Wabi-Sabi & Ma (Kengo Kuma, Junichiro Tanizaki)
- **Philosophy:** Beauty in imperfection, the evocative power of shadows (*In Praise of Shadows*), negative space (*Ma*).
- **Palette:** Charcoal, weathered cedar, unbleached linen, smoked tea, tarnished brass.
- **Typography:** Quiet, generous letter-spaced serif or refined Japanese-inspired sans (Shippori Mincho).
- **Layout:** Vast breathing voids (70%+ negative space), subtle vertical text accents, organic asymmetry.

## 6. Industrial Brutalism (Le Corbusier, Alison & Peter Smithson)
- **Philosophy:** Raw mechanical honesty (*béton brut*), exposed structure, anti-bourgeois utility.
- **Palette:** Raw poured concrete gray, dark asphalt, hazard amber/safety orange accent.
- **Typography:** Monospaced and heavy industrial grotesque (Monument Extended, Space Mono).
- **Layout:** Rigid exposed border wires, monolithic block containers, technical metadata readouts.

## 7. Dieter Rams Functionalism (Braun, Vitsœ)
- **Philosophy:** "Less, but better." Design is unobtrusive, honest, long-lasting, and meticulous down to the last detail.
- **Palette:** Matte white, warm light gray, muted slate, single precision green or orange indicator.
- **Typography:** Neutral grotesque (Univers, Inter Display, SF Pro) with perfect optical sizing.
- **Layout:** Modular bento grids with uniform corner radii, physical dial and toggle affordances.

## 8. Kinetic Auteur & Avant-Garde (El Lissitzky, Dziga Vertov)
- **Philosophy:** Dynamic cinematic staging, motion as message, high narrative tension.
- **Palette:** High-contrast black and bone white with electric crimson or cobalt slash.
- **Typography:** Dramatic scale contrast (clamp 10vw hero against 0.75rem micro-data).
- **Layout:** Dynamic diagonal shear vectors, scroll-triggered camera perspective shifts.

## 9. Organic Biomimicry (Santiago Calatrava, Antoni Gaudí)
- **Philosophy:** Structural forms inspired by skeletal mechanics, natural growth vectors, and fluid dynamics.
- **Palette:** Bone white, deep ocean teal, leaf canopy emerald, sandstone beige.
- **Typography:** Fluid humanist serif or sculpted organic sans.
- **Layout:** Continuous curved spine, Fibonacci branching cards, smooth spring physics.

## 10. Cybernetic Obsidian (Dark High-Tech Terminal)
- **Philosophy:** Precision instruments, aerospace telemetry, high-information density.
- **Palette:** Deep space obsidian (`oklch(0.10 0.01 260)`), phosphor green or electric cyan accent.
- **Typography:** High-legibility monospaced data type paired with neutral UI sans.
- **Layout:** Compact modular telemetry panels, live status pings, micro-contrast divider rules.

## 11. Tactile Editorial (Museum Archive, Physical Printmaking)
- **Philosophy:** The permanence of ink on paper, tactile craft, literary depth.
- **Palette:** Deep printer's ink (`#1c1917`), deckle-edge cream (`#fafaf9`), rich burgundy accent.
- **Typography:** High-contrast transitional serif (Newsreader, Playfair, Freight Text) with generous leading.
- **Layout:** Wide margins, drop caps, pull quotes set in large italics, generous column gutters.

## 12. Postmodern Memphis (Ettore Sottsass, Studio Alchimia)
- **Philosophy:** Joyful rebellion against strict modernist dogmatism, vibrant geometry, tactile whimsy.
- **Palette:** Pastel mint, electric coral, cobalt, lemon yellow, black-and-white squiggles.
- **Typography:** Playful geometric sans with character (Syne, Outfit, Recoleta).
- **Layout:** Layered asymmetric shapes, overlapping planes, spirited geometric patterns.
