# Cross-Medium Mapping

All 7 Ars pillars translated into concrete moves for **writing/editorial**, **motion/film**, **sound/music**, and **spatial/product** work — not only screens.

This document exists because the original skill only had operational content for screen/UI work. Every pillar now has a move for every major creative medium.

---

## Pillar 1: Concetto — Governing Thesis

| Medium | What the Concetto controls |
|:---|:---|
| **Screen / UI** | Every color token, spacing unit, and component. If a button can't justify itself against the Concetto, it's removed. |
| **Writing / Editorial** | The single governing argument or emotional tension the entire piece earns. Every paragraph either advances or contextualizes it — nothing else. |
| **Motion / Film** | The through-line of the edit. Shot selection, color grade, score mood, and title card language are all consequent. A film about grief that opens with a sunny establishing shot violates the Concetto. |
| **Sound / Music** | The tonal and textural character: a drone that implies geological time, a sparse piano that implies intimacy, a polyrhythm that implies entropy. Every instrument choice and processing decision either reinforces or contradicts it. |
| **Spatial / Product** | The material narrative: "machined precision," "reclaimed imperfection," "monastic economy." Every surface finish, joint detail, and dimensional proportion either holds or breaks the thesis. |

## Pillar 2: Saper Vedere — Perception Choreography

| Medium | Apex (Punctum) | Flow path | Sanctuary |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Screen / UI** | Maximum contrast, sharpest edge, semantic weight | Diagonal vector from apex to secondary CTA | Generous negative space — no element every competing at equal weight |
| **Writing** | The plainest, most precisely chosen sentence in the piece (often near the start or at a structural turn) | Topic sentences that carry the argument forward; transitions that re-anchor the reader | Short paragraphs and single-sentence paragraphs as breath between dense argument |
| **Motion / Film** | The one frame or cut where emotion peaks — the held close-up, the deliberate pause before the reveal | Scene rhythm: rising cut frequency leading to the apex; deceleration after | Silence, a held wide shot, or a textural ambient passage that clears cognitive load |
| **Sound** | The peak dynamic or most exposed melodic statement | Gradual textural build or breakdown leading to and from the apex | Near-silence, a single sustained note, or a room tone passage |
| **Spatial / Product** | The primary handling point or the surface detail that rewards closest inspection | Circulation path: how the eye or body moves from entry to the apex | Empty planes, unoccupied volume, material restraint that makes the apex land |

## Pillar 3: Dynamic Armature — Proportional Systems

| Medium | Proportional system | Application |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Screen / UI** | Golden Section (Φ = 1.618), Root-2 grids, diatonic spacing scale | Column widths, section heights, spacing tokens, type scale |
| **Writing** | Paragraph ratio: long exposition / short declarative / single-sentence punch mirrors visual mass ratios. Sentence length variability follows a rhythm pattern (long–long–short or long–short–long). | Structural pacing across the entire piece, not word-level choices |
| **Motion / Film** | Scene duration ratios (short–long–short), act proportions (setup : development : resolution ≈ 1 : 2 : 1 or 1 : 1 : 0.618 for compressed endings) | Cut timing, scene length planning in the edit |
| **Sound** | Phrase lengths in bars, structural section proportions (intro : verse : chorus : bridge), dynamic arc timing | Arrangement and compositional structure |
| **Spatial / Product** | Root rectangle section proportions applied to plan/elevation dimensions; diatonic spacing applied to gap and setback relationships | Form proportions, joinery details, product ergonomic spacing |

## Pillar 4: Chiaroscuro & Sfumato — Light, Value, and Depth

| Medium | Value apex (specular) | Mid-values | Depth/void | Sfumato equivalent |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Screen / UI** | Peak-luminance OKLCH accent on focal element | Neutral surface hierarchy in OKLCH midrange | Dark atmospheric ground | Blur/opacity falloff on peripheral UI elements |
| **Writing** | The single most concrete, specific, highest-image-density sentence | Explanatory prose in mid-register | Abstract framing sentences at section edges | Sentence rhythm trailing off into white space at section endings |
| **Motion / Film** | The held frame at peak emotion | Scene-average exposure and grade | Underexposed depth or black crush in backgrounds | Rack focus, gentle vignette, or atmospheric haze on edges |
| **Sound** | Peak dynamic — the loudest, most harmonically saturated moment | Mid-energy passages: textured but not maximal | Near-silence, room tone, or long decay tail | Reverb tail, high-frequency rolloff on distant elements, gentle fade-out of peripheral elements |
| **Spatial / Product** | The material or finish detail that catches light first | Matte surfaces and mid-tone transitions | Dark recesses, shadow gaps, unlit internal volumes | Chamfers, radii, and transitional surfaces that soften hard edges |

## Pillar 5: Contrapposto — Dynamic Asymmetrical Equilibrium

| Medium | Dense mass (active flank) | Void (counterweight flank) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Screen / UI** | Primary content column, complex component | Charged negative space; a single small element at the far edge anchors the void |
| **Writing** | Dense argumentative passage, evidence-heavy paragraph | White space, short paragraph, deliberate sentence fragment for breath |
| **Motion / Film** | Action-heavy scene, dense exposition sequence | Quiet observational scene, lingering wide shot |
| **Sound** | Full-ensemble passage or textural peak | Solo instrument passage, near-silence, or stripped arrangement |
| **Spatial / Product** | Massed structural element or dense material cluster | Void space, open plan, minimal slab or surface |

---

## Pillar 6: Biomimetic Skeleton — Structure Before Surface

| Medium | Skeleton (must be complete first) | Skin (applied after) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Screen / UI** | Information topology, component hierarchy, interaction model | Colors, gradients, micro-animations, font choices |
| **Writing** | Argument structure: thesis → evidence → counterargument → resolution | Prose style, word choice, metaphor, sentence variety |
| **Motion / Film** | Scene logic, shot list, edit structure, narrative arc | Color grade, score, sound design, titles |
| **Sound** | Harmonic structure, arrangement, rhythmic skeleton | Timbre, processing, mixing decisions |
| **Spatial / Product** | Structural system, circulation, load paths, primary form | Surface finish, material texture, detail joinery |

## Pillar 7: Relational Color & Perceptual Economy

**Note:** The ~90/8/2 ratio is an original analytical framework. It is not a specific doctrine from Itten or Goethe (both of whom wrote about color relationships generally, not this specific ratio).

| Medium | ~90% Atmospheric Ground | ~8% Structural Harmony | ~2% Chroma Strike |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Screen / UI** | Low-chroma neutral or tinted base surfaces | Supporting secondary hues and tonal variants | Single saturated accent on the primary action |
| **Writing** | Plain, concrete, precise prose — the baseline register | Elevated lexical register at transitions and sub-thesis statements | The single most charged metaphor, the single most unexpected word — used once |
| **Motion / Film** | Neutral or lightly graded ambient scenes | Moderate tonal shift in supporting scenes | Single high-saturation, high-contrast, peak-moment frame or sequence |
| **Sound** | Ambient or mid-energy textural ground | Secondary melodic or harmonic material | Single exposed melodic statement or dynamic peak |
| **Spatial / Product** | Primary material in its natural or neutral finish | Secondary material or surface treatment in supporting volumes | Single contrasting material accent, one detail finish that rewards close inspection |
