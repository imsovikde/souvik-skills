# The Miesian Crucible: Subtractive Reduction & Structural Honesty

> *"Less is more."* — Ludwig Mies van der Rohe  
> *"Good design is as little design as possible."* — Dieter Rams

---

## 1. The 30% Reduction Mandate (`/ars:cut`)

AI coding agents instinctively add visual noise: unnecessary containers, card wrappers around card wrappers, redundant eyebrow labels, gratuitous icons, and decorative borders.

Whenever `/ars:cut` or final refinement is executed:
1. **Identify Every Visual Container:** Ask of each box, card, border, and divider: *"If this border is deleted, does the information collapse?"* If no, delete it immediately.
2. **Replace Borders with Spatial Distance:** Use negative space and typographic scale rather than 1px lines to establish boundaries.
3. **Eliminate Redundant Adjectives & Eyebrows:** Delete arbitrary uppercase kickers (e.g. `FEATURES`, `OVERVIEW`, `01 / 02 / 03`) when the content itself establishes context.
4. **Mandatory 30% Elimination:** At least 30% of unnecessary decorative elements must be purged before shipping.

---

## 2. Structural Honesty

- Never fake depth with arbitrary drop shadows that contradict the light source.
- Never use cards inside cards.
- Never wrap a 2-line sentence in a full-bleed glowing container.
