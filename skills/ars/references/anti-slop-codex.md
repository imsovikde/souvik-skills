# The Anti-Slop Codex: Deterministic Prohibitions & Master Replacements

## The 10 Absolute Prohibitions with Side-by-Side Replacements

| # | Banned AI Pattern (Slop) | Master Artistic Replacement (Ars) | Rationale |
|:--|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | **Purple/Indigo AI Gradients** (`#8b5cf6` → `#3b82f6`) | Single-hue OKLCH atmospheric falloff (`oklch(0.95 0.02 55)` → `oklch(0.12 0.01 260)`). | Avoids the generic 2024–2026 AI template look. |
| 2 | **Cards-Inside-Cards** | Flat hierarchy with harmonic scale and generous whitespace. | Eliminates visual claustrophobia and container nesting. |
| 3 | **Default Glassmorphism** (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` everywhere) | Solid matte surfaces with multi-layered directional shadow elevation. | Preserves contrast, readability, and performance. |
| 4 | **The "Three Equal Cards" Template** | Asymmetric grid with varied cell scales based on root rectangles (√2, √3). | Breaks monotonous repetition and introduces visual rhythm. |
| 5 | **Overused AI Font Pairings** (Inter + Slate-900 / Default Fraunces without intention) | Distinctive contrast-axis pairings chosen for *this specific project's register* (Newsreader / Plus Jakarta, Monument Mono / Neue Haas, Cormorant / Söhne, etc.). | Establishes authentic brand voice and typographic personality. |
| 6 | **Eyebrow Kickers on Every Section** | Varied section openings; allow display typography to command authority. | Eliminates bureaucratic micro-label clutter. |
| 7 | **Decorative Pill Badges** ("✨ AI Powered Magic") | Concrete factual claims set in refined body typography. | Replaces marketing fluff with technical credibility. |
| 8 | **Arbitrary Padding Values** (any non-harmonic pixel value) | Strict adherence to the chosen harmonic spacing scale (`--space-s`, `--space-m`, `--space-l`, etc.). | Enforces mathematical visual harmony. |
| 9 | **Dead Center Alignment** (Hero text, stats, CTAs centered in a row) | Poised asymmetrical Contrapposto with intentional dynamic tension. | Replaces static stiffness with kinetic equilibrium. |
| 10 | **Low-Contrast Gray-on-Dark Text** (Luminance < 4.5:1) | Calibrated 5-tier luminance scale with WCAG AAA core contrast. | Guarantees accessibility without sacrificing atmosphere. |

## Second-Order Slop: Anti-Slop Fixes That Became Defaults

Anti-slop treatments have their own lifecycle — they get adopted, overused, and become the next wave of generic output. These are currently second-order slop as of 2025–2026:

| Second-Order Slop Pattern | Why it became slop | Replacement |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Instrument Serif as the "distinctive" serif** | Instrument Serif became one of the most recognizable "AI tell" font choices in the design skill ecosystem, used as the go-to distinctive alternative — which made it un-distinctive. | Choose a serif based on the *specific project register*, not because it breaks from Inter. Options with lower saturation: Newsreader, Freight Display, Canela, or a variable serif matching the project's historical lineage. |
| **Radical asymmetry as a default posture** | Asymmetry used reflexively is just a different form of template-thinking. A fintech dashboard forced into Contrapposto layout reads as arbitrary. | Apply asymmetry *when the Concetto calls for dynamic tension*. Use restrained symmetry when the brief demands institutional authority or calm precision. |
| **OKLCH-everywhere as a sophistication signal** | Specifying OKLCH values in a context where the team uses standard hex tooling creates friction without benefit, and signals awareness of OKLCH rather than genuine use of it. | Use OKLCH in stylesheets where perceptual uniformity matters across a gradient or palette. Default to the format the project's actual toolchain supports. |
| **"Kinetic" as a Concetto word** | "Kinetic" became a default Concetto placeholder — meaningless without specific physical referent. | Ground metaphors in specific material or physical phenomena: "the surface tension of water before it breaks," "the moment a tungsten filament goes white." |
| **The anti-template grid with 7 unequal columns** | Asymmetric grids used for novelty rather than content requirements create a new rigidity — the rigidity of forced irregularity. | Let content mass and structural hierarchy determine grid divisions, not aesthetic novelty. |

## Medium-Specific Slop Patterns

### Writing / Editorial
| Banned | Replacement |
|:---|:---|
| Starting every paragraph with "In a world where..." | Open *in medias res* with a specific, concrete image or claim |
| Stacking three consecutive rhetorical questions | Use one, precisely placed, then answer it — or don't ask it |
| The "not just X, but Y" sentence structure (overused in marketing copy) | Specificity: name the actual thing without the contrastive frame |
| Abstract value words without evidence ("innovative," "transformative," "seamless") | Concrete, specific, verifiable claims |

### Motion / Film
| Banned | Replacement |
|:---|:---|
| Zoom + lens flare on every emotional peak | Restraint at the peak — hold the shot, let the performance carry it |
| Moody blue-orange split toning as the default grade | Choose a grade derived from the film's color Concetto |
| Rapid-cut montage as default for "energy" | Vary rhythm: slow cuts build dread; fast cuts build chaos — neither is "more" |

### Sound / Music
| Banned | Replacement |
|:---|:---|
| Reverb-soaked pads as default "atmosphere" | Choose the acoustic environment the piece inhabits and build toward it |
| Sidechain pump on every electronic track | Sidechain only when rhythmic pumping is the *point* of the texture |
| The major-to-minor key change as the emotional "reveal" | Find the specific harmonic or timbral move the piece earns |
